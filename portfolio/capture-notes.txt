       the same build deployed at https://sericumvia1.sepafshar.com
Captured from: the site/ folder of that commit, served locally (http://localhost)
Browser: Google Chrome, headless (new headless mode), English (en-US), no dev tools, no cursor, no scrollbars

Viewports
- Desktop: 1440 x 900 CSS px at device scale 2 -> 2880 x 1800 PNG
- Phone: 390 x 844 CSS px at device scale 3 -> 1170 x 2532 PNG, iPhone user agent, touch events enabled.
  Headless Chrome will not make a window narrower than about 500 px, so the page ran inside an exact
  390 x 844 frame and the screenshot was cropped to that frame. The page saw a true 390 px viewport.

How the screens were reached
- The game was played by clicking its own links in order (for example Travel, Continue, Continue,
  1.Cold and mountainous ...). A small capture-only script did the clicking. It also skipped the
  once-per-session loading screen and turned off the page fade-in, so no frame was caught mid-fade.
- Game text, maps, images and numbers were not changed. Every number shown comes from that play-through.
- The ending path: Fall, Turkey, bought 10 food, Green route, bought 1 saffron, looked around at the
  river, rented a boat, reached the caravanserai. Final screen: 650 coins, 8 food, 1 saffron,
  70 health, 64 days left, score 1668.

Frames
- The browser and phone frames are drawn plainly, with no text, no URL and no logos. The address bar is left blank.
- The parchment background is generated (grain, soft sepia stains, faint fibres, vignette), with no text.
- Every screenshot sits inside the central 80% of its image (at least 10% margin on each side).

Key art (added 2026-11-11; composited only from the game's own artwork, nothing invented, no text)
Built with Python and Pillow (_tools/keyart.py). Pixel art is used at 1x or exactly 1/2, nearest-neighbour only.
- card-1200x750.png   KEY ART. Built from: source/assets/main-map.png (pixel map of Iran, used at 1x; the white
                      background made transparent, the grey route lines recoloured gold with a soft gold glow behind
                      them, land colours softened 22% toward parchment; red caravanserai markers unchanged) and
                      source/assets/man-cammel.png (pixel camel and merchant, 1x), on the generated parchment
                      used for the screenshot frames, with a soft shadow under the sprite.
- og-1200x630.png     KEY ART. Same layers and scale as the card, on a wider 1.9:1 parchment.
- store-720x720.png   KEY ART. Built from: The Sericum Via 1.png (painted cover art, 1024x1024). The title
                      "Iran's Caravanserais", the map labels (Tabriz, Ardebil, Qazvin, Hamadan, Ispahan, Iran) and
                      the dotted route were removed by refilling those areas from the surrounding paper only (no
                      drawing added). Cropped to the left caravanserai and the caravan (cover pixels x 0-588,
                      y 430-1024), resized to 554x560 (smooth resampling, as it is painted art, not pixel art), and
                      faded at the edges into parchment tinted to the cover's own paper colour.
- icon-192x192.png    KEY ART. Built from: source/assets/man-cammel.png at exactly 1/2 size, nearest-neighbour
                      (106x109), centred on plain parchment. Checked at 192, 64 and 32 px.
All four keep their subject inside the central 80%. Card/OG and store are different compositions.
